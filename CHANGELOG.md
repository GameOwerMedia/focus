## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- `focus` skill: 10 everyday users and 10 decision-makers in one pass, with a
  nine-part joint synthesis.
- `lite` mode: 3 + 3 personas and the top 5 changes.
- Pluggable market files with a `world` default and a template for new markets.
- Example conversation with real outputs for full and lite runs, verbatim except
  for one renamed persona in each (noted at the top of each file).
- Structure tests and CI on Linux, Windows and macOS.
