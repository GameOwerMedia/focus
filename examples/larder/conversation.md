# Example conversation: Larder

This is the exact user message used for the example runs in this folder. Larder
is a fictional product; its demo website lives in the
[focus-swarm](https://github.com/GameOwerMedia/focus-swarm/tree/main/examples/demo-site)
repository.

---

> We're about to launch Larder, a shared pantry app for households. One live
> list of what is in the fridge and cupboards, shared with everyone you live
> with. You add items by scanning a barcode or a supermarket receipt, it nudges
> you the day before something expires with three recipes that use it up, and
> it builds the next shopping list from what is running low.
>
> Pricing: Starter is free (1 member, 50 items). Household is $3.99/month billed
> annually ($47.88/year) or $5.99 monthly, up to 5 members. Family+ is $6.99/month
> billed annually, up to 10 members, adds a waste report and meal planner. The
> 14-day trial needs a card and converts to the annual plan.
>
> Web app works today, iOS "soon", Android later this year. Receipt scanning
> works for most US and UK supermarkets. We also plan "partner offers":
> discounts from retail partners based on what is in your pantry, funded by
> sharing anonymised, aggregated shopping insights with those partners.
>
> Two founders, 400 beta households, raising a pre-seed round next quarter.
> /focus
