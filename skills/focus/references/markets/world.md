# Market: world

The default market. A product judged by a worldwide audience and by global
decision-makers.

## Detect

Use `world` when the product is in English, priced in USD, EUR or GBP, sold
through global stores or platforms, or when nothing in the conversation ties it
to a single country. A team based in one country that builds for a global
audience is still `world`.

## Language and style

- Output entirely in English.
- Personas may use phrases from their own language and the English of a
  non-native speaker where it fits; do not caricature.
- Prices in USD by default, EUR or GBP when the product prices that way.

## Panel A

- Spread across continents and income levels: North America, Western and
  Eastern Europe, Latin America, South Asia, East and Southeast Asia, Africa,
  the Middle East. Locals and migrants.
- At least one non-native English speaker using the product in English, and at
  least one person on a low-end Android phone or a slow connection.
- Everyday habits to draw on: App Store and Google Play, Steam, YouTube, TikTok,
  Instagram, WhatsApp, Reddit, Discord, Amazon, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google
  Pay, local super-apps where relevant, prepaid mobile data, shared family
  accounts, second jobs and gig work.

## Panel B

Match every seat to the product's domain. The organisations below are public
examples of the kind of seat, not a required list; Panel B personas are
fictional people even when they sit at one of these.

- **The Money:** global VC firms and accelerators (for example a16z, Sequoia,
  Index Ventures, Accel, Balderton, Atomico, Y Combinator, Techstars),
  corporate venture arms, angel syndicates, revenue-based financing; public and
  non-dilutive money (EIC Accelerator, Horizon Europe, Creative Europe MEDIA,
  national film and arts funds, Sundance Institute, Doc Society); strategic
  acquirers.
- **The Gatekeepers:** app store and platform editorial teams (Apple, Google,
  Steam, Netflix, YouTube, Spotify, TikTok), tech and trade press (TechCrunch,
  The Verge, Wired, Variety, Deadline), Product Hunt, industry analysts
  (Gartner, Forrester), festival and conference programmers (Sundance, SXSW,
  Berlinale, Cannes, Annecy, Web Summit), retail and marketplace category
  buyers.
- **The Operators, Strategic Minds and Domain Expert:** executives of a
  comparable global company, a partner at a global consultancy, a sector
  banker, the person who built the current category leader.

## Rules and regulators

Raise only the ones that actually touch the product: GDPR and UK GDPR, CCPA and
other US state privacy laws, the EU AI Act, the Digital Services Act and
Digital Markets Act, COPPA and age-appropriate design codes for anything a
child could use, accessibility law (ADA, European Accessibility Act), app store
review and payment rules, consumer law on subscriptions and auto-renewal,
copyright and licensing by market, sector regulators (finance, health,
gambling, telecoms).

## Benchmarks

Benchmark against the global category leaders first, then against the free
workaround people already use (a spreadsheet, a group chat, a notes app,
"nothing").

## Labels

- Panel A verdicts: YES / NO / MAYBE
- Panel B verdicts: FUND / CONDITIONAL / PASS
- Headings as in `SKILL.md`.
